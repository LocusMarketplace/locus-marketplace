# Locus brand affinity and catalog expansion (staging)

This release is still an independent, noindex proof of concept, not a retailer partnership or commercial launch.

## Catalog and rights

- `src/data/catalog.js` and `src/data/expansion.js` define the expanded editorial brand taxonomy and synthetic, generic product archetypes. Concept names, prices, fit, materials and assortments are illustrative, not a representation of retailer inventory. Concept links go only to the merchant's home page. No affiliate links are fabricated.
- `src/data/references.js` contains 11 manually reviewed Buck Mason product reference snapshots; each records its own source URL and date. The merchant explicitly publishes an agent-readable catalog at https://www.buckmason.com/llms.txt and https://www.buckmason.com/for-ai. The added items were reviewed in the merchant's fall and November collection pages and individual product pages. No merchant photos or product-description prose were copied, nor was an automated crawler run. Published agent-readable content does not automatically grant commercial republication permission; get authorization before scaling or launch.
- The product illustrations and abstract brand cards are original code-native graphics, not retailer logos, photographs or faithful depictions of actual merchandise. The source label remains visible on every item.
- Future authorized feed records can coexist with concept and editorial-reference records. The existing feed rights gate remains unchanged; retailer images and product descriptions require suitable display rights.

## Matrix and ranking

Version 2 adds explicit profiles for all 55 brands (`src/data/brandProfiles.js`): three ranked aesthetic worlds, occasion/use cases, palette, silhouette/design language, editorial narrative, existing style tags, department and indicative price tier. Ten worlds span coastal ease, modern heritage, modern prep & club, quiet minimalism, active & elevated, romantic & eclectic, refined everyday, outdoor & utility, warm organic living, and sculptural modern. They overlap intentionally; brand classifications are hypotheses and not product-level attributes, current assortment claims or retailer-provided metadata.

The engine supports 1,485 distinct unordered pairs. Direct affinities require at least one shared department and world. Their score weights ranked world overlap (40%), style tags (15%), occasions (15%), palette (10%), silhouettes/design (8%), price (7%) and category (5%). World overlap uses weighted Jaccard with primary/secondary/tertiary weights 1/.65/.35; other categorical overlap uses Dice; price is tier distance. Lifestyle bridges require disjoint departments, at least one shared world and palette cue, and score world (65%), palette (20%), style (10%) and price (5%). They are explicitly not substitutes. All connections uses the applicable model. Pair scores are symmetric, 0–100, not probabilities; suggestions exclude scores below 25. Multiple anchors are blended by weighting the best pair 65% and the mean pair score 35%, including zero matches. These provisional weights warrant editorial calibration and eventual privacy-preserving empirical validation. Neither score measures actual customer overlap, objective quality or conversion probability.

The Brand matrix supports four anchors, three connection modes, department/world/occasion/price positioning filters, explanation cards, interactive pairwise table and a seven-dimensional assessment for any two brands. The resulting edit can be shopped directly, as can any selected pair. The homepage and brand directory expose the worlds, and the shop supports world and occasion filters, constrained multi-brand edits, and aesthetic phrases such as “quiet luxury sweater” or “modern farmhouse table lamp.” Existing brand-name, similar-brand and multi-query searches preserve product and price constraints. Recommended ranking uses locally declared preferences, worlds, saved items and query brand mentions. It does not collect demographics or infer identity. Results load in batches of 24.

## Next phase

Once meaningful consented, sufficiently aggregated behavior is available, evaluate editorial affinity against observed co-consideration and category-level demand; show confidence and suppress small cohorts. Do not represent the present editorial scores as empirical, and never claim visits are sales. Maintain the prelaunch safeguards in `docs/PROOF_OF_CONCEPT_GUARDRAILS.md`.
