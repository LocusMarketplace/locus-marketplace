# Locus partner proof of concept — rights and privacy controls

This is a risk-reduction implementation, not a legal opinion or a guarantee against claims. Obtain US IP/privacy counsel review before a public commercial rollout or shared consumer-data program.

## Rights and sources

- Brand names are plain-text identifiers, not logos; no implied endorsement, affiliation, or approved seller status. Concept titles, prices and graphics are illustrative. Ordinary homepage links go to the brands rather than framing or copying their sites.
- The small Buck Mason editorial pilot uses factual names, materials, prices, colors, generic style tags and canonical product links from its explicitly published agent-readable catalog (https://www.buckmason.com/llms.txt, reviewed 2026-11-21). It does not copy prose or images. Agent-readable access does not grant a commercial republication license; verify terms and obtain approval before scaling it. Snapshots are dated, not live offers. No automated extraction runs.
- Source pages are recorded per reference in `src/data/references.js`. To remove the pilot, remove those records; no merchant assets are stored.
- Faherty's terms prohibit scraping and commercial content reuse (https://fahertybrand.com/pages/terms-of-use). Quince's terms prohibit scraping (https://www.quince.com/terms). Reformation expressly restricts deep links without authorization (https://www.thereformation.com/terms-and-conditions.html). These remain concept records with ordinary homepage references, not copied retailer records. Review each merchant's terms and permission before any expanded data or deep-link integration.
- Do not ingest, hotlink, or recreate retailer photography, descriptions, logos, reviews, or branded visual assets without suitable rights. General access to a website, public HTML or an API is not a grant of rights. The demo uses original CSS artwork, generic icons and system fonts. The separate authorized-feed pipeline remains gated by merchant rights.
- Before public launch: record approval scope, permitted display fields, affiliate/analytics arrangements and expiration; implement takedown and correction workflows; confirm Locus trademark clearance; provide a contact channel, review terms of service and disclosures, and verify prices/availability. This demo is marked noindex, but its workers.dev URL is not access controlled.

## Consumer-intent data

- Optional demo counters are OFF on each page load. They stay in client memory and are never transmitted to an analytics endpoint. Disabling clears them; refresh also clears. The only dimensions are a fixed event type, catalog category, known brand, catalog source and broad item-price band. No raw search strings, names, emails, session IDs, IP addresses, precise location, age, tracking cookies, fingerprints or retailer checkout information are added to the counters.
- Existing saved products and declared style preferences are stored locally for the shopper; not sent to a database. Cloudflare and retailer sites may independently process requests under their own policies.
- Search, save, compare and outbound click counts are intent signals. Clicking a brand/product does not establish a purchase or conversion. Sales outcomes require a consented feedback loop and retailer/affiliate reporting agreements. No data is sold or shared by this demo.
- Before shared analytics: define purpose, notice, retention/deletion, access controls and user rights; review applicable state/international privacy and children's privacy rules; aggregate across sufficiently large cohorts, suppress small groups, and avoid sensitive inference or identity linkage. Test privacy controls; never sell individual behavioral histories or reidentify cohorts. Partner reports should distinguish concepts, editorial references and approved real inventory, and should not misstate clicks as unique people or sales.
